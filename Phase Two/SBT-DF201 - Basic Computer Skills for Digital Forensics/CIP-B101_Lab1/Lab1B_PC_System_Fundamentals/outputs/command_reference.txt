=================================================================
PART C: DISK SECTOR CALCULATIONS
=================================================================

No screenshot needed - this is a written calculation in your report
Formula: Disk Size = number_of_sectors x bytes_per_sector
To convert to GB: divide by 1024 x 1024 x 1024

For YOUR disk, find sector count from:
  wmic diskdrive get size,totalsectors,bytespersector
