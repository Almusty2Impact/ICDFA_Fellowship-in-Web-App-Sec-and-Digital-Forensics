=== TASK E: Endianness and Data Stored in Memory ===

--- System Byte Order ---
sys.byteorder = little
Architecture: x86-64 (Kali Linux)

--- Interpretation Comparison ---
Raw Hex Bytes  | Little-Endian     | Big-Endian        | Explanation
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
01000000       | 1                 | 16,777,216        | 01 is LSB (LE) vs MSB (BE)
FF000000       | 255               | 4,278,190,080     | FF=255 in LSB vs ~4GB in MSB
00000100       | 65,536            | 256               | 01 byte at different positions
78563412       | 305,419,896       | 2,018,915,346     | 6.6x difference

--- Forensic Relevance ---
The same four bytes produce different values depending on byte order.
MBR partition tables use little-endian. Network protocols (TCP/IP) use big-endian.
Misinterpreting endianness can lead to wrong file sizes, timestamps, or addresses.
