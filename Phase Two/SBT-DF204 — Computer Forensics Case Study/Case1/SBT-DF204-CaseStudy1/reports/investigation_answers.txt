Q8: What conclusion can you defend?
---------------------------------------------------------------
CONCLUSION (Confidence: HIGH):
The device at 192.168.15.4 (MAC 00:17:f2:e2:c0:ce) submitted the harassment message 
to www.willselfdestruct.com at 2008-07-22 02:04:24 EDT. The same device was 
concurrently logged into Gmail as jcoachj@gmail.com, which corresponds to 
Johnny Coach, a student on the Chemistry 109 roster.

LIMITATIONS:
1. Shared open wireless network: Multiple users share 192.168.15.4 (NAT).
2. The MAC address alone cannot prove identity; session cookies provide the link.
3. The Facebook cookie (beth@bethr.org) on the same network shows another user was active.
4. No direct forensic examination of the physical device was possible.
5. The User-Agent (IE 6.0 on Windows XP) does not match the Apple MAC prefix (00:17:f2) — 
   possible MAC spoofing or virtualization, though the MAC was consistent.

ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS:
- Johnny Coach may have loaned the device to someone else.
- Another user may have accessed the Gmail session on the same device.
- The MAC could have been spoofed.
However, none of these alternatives are supported by the captured evidence; the 
simplest explanation (Johnny Coach sent the message) is the most defensible.
